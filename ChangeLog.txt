AuctionDB (AHDB) - WoW Forever edition

v2.0.0 (unreleased)
- Rewrite for WoW Forever (new C_AuctionHouse API), no libraries; Classic/Mists/retail code is on the legacy branch.
- Full AH scan when you open the AH (Blizzard allows one every 15 minutes per game session; hold Shift to skip),
  "AHDB Scan" button above the AH with the time left before the next scan.
- Price history per item (min, median, quantity) kept separately for the Alliance, Horde and neutral auction houses.
- Item tooltips: vendor price per unit (the game's line is for the whole stack), last scan's AH min and median.
- Warns on the AH sell page when your price, after the AH cut, is less than what a vendor pays.
- Learns items vendors refuse although they have a sell price (e.g. Instant Poison), /ahdb unsellable.
- Options panel (Options > AddOns > AHDB, /ahdb options), /ahdb price <item> for offline price history,
  /ahdb bug for a copyable log.
